    About the data features

    Age
    The patient's age in years, calculated from the birth year.
    Sex
    Male is entered as 1 and Female as 0, the usual coding of this dataset.
    Chest pain type (cp)
    0 Typical angina, 1 Atypical angina, 2 Non-anginal pain, 3 Asymptomatic.
    Resting blood pressure (trestbps)
    Blood pressure at rest, in mm Hg.
    Cholesterol (chol)
    Serum cholesterol, in mg/dl.
    Fasting blood sugar (fbs)
    1 if fasting blood sugar is above 120 mg/dl, otherwise 0.
    Resting ECG (restecg)
    A coded result of the resting electrocardiogram (0, 1 or 2). The meaning of each code is not stated here because it has not been verified for this project.
    Maximum heart rate (thalach)
    The highest heart rate reached, in beats per minute.
    Exercise-induced angina (exang)
    1 if exercise brought on chest pain, otherwise 0.
    Oldpeak
    ST depression during exercise compared with rest.
    Slope
    Slope of the peak-exercise ST segment: 0 Upsloping, 1 Flat, 2 Downsloping.
    Major vessels (ca)
    The number of major vessels highlighted by a scan. The choices shown are the values found in your heart.csv.
    Thal
    A coded result of a thalassemia-related test. The choices are the codes found in your heart.csv; their meanings depend on the dataset's encoding and are not asserted here.
